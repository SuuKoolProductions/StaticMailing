18 U.S.C. §§ 2257 and 2257A — Content Compliance Statement

Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble, operates a digital creator platform that permits illustrated and animated adult content featuring fictional adult characters.

Shobble does not permit sexually explicit photographs, live-action videos, or other sexual depictions of humans. That prohibition includes an actual human performer, an identifiable real individual, a drawn human, and an AI-generated human. Source files do not authorize those depictions.

A sexual drawing of a fictional non-human character that Shobble classifies as AI-generated is not published. The creator may submit source files for human review of that file. Acceptance of those files does not authorize a sexual depiction of a human.

The adult content permitted without that review consists of fictional illustrations and animation of fictional adult characters that are not human and are not classified as AI-generated, created without the participation of actual human performers. Accordingly, such material does not ordinarily fall within the performer identification and recordkeeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. §§ 2257 and 2257A.

Shobble maintains content moderation, reporting, removal procedures, and age and identity verification requirements for creators seeking monetization.

Any content that falls outside these permitted categories is subject to rejection or removal under Shobble's applicable policies.

This statement describes Shobble's permitted content categories and does not claim an exemption for material that would otherwise be subject to federal recordkeeping requirements.
