SHOBBLE COMPLAINTS AND CONTENT REMOVAL POLICY

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Contact: team@shobble.com

1. How to complain

1.1 On adult.shobble.com and adult.dev.shobble.com, open the content menu or the content page and choose Report / Request Removal. You can report illegal content, a violation of platform standards, or ask for the content to be removed.

1.2 The same form explains that Shobble reviews and resolves these complaints within five business days. Business days skip Saturday and Sunday. The moderation record stores that deadline.

2. Review

2.1 Shobble reads the report, the content, and any note you included. The case stays in the moderation queue until it is resolved or dismissed.

2.2 Outcomes can include no action, a warning, hiding the content, or removing it. Shobble tells the reporter that a decision can be appealed.

2.3 To appeal a decision about your own content, use Settings → Moderation, or email team@shobble.com. Appeals already available under the Community Content Guidelines and the Acceptable Use Policy still apply. This policy does not replace the DMCA Policy for copyright notices.

3. Depicted persons

3.1 A request from a person who says they are depicted in the content is not an ordinary five-business-day complaint. Use the separate choice in the report form. That process is described in the Depicted Person Removal Policy. Automatic hiding from that form is limited to five distinct listings for a signed-in reporter in 24 hours. A person without an account can still submit that request for urgent review, and it does not hide the listing by itself. A legal preservation hold keeps the files and does not by itself keep the listing hidden. The limit does not apply to an illegal-content report or a child-safety report.
