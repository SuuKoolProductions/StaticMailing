SHOBBL EVENT & COLLABORATIVE JAM POLICY

Effective Date: November 16, 2026
Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Event & Collaborative Jam Policy (“Event Policy”) governs the creation, hosting, participation, distribution, recording, and monetization of Events on the Shobbl platform (“Platform”).

This Policy applies to all users who organize, host, participate in, contribute to, or attend Events, including collaborative creation activities and live, semi-live, or scheduled digital performances. It operates in conjunction with the Master Terms of Use, Creator Content License & Rights Framework, Content Classification Policy, AI Use-Case Policy, Monetization Addenda, and any applicable Event-specific rules. In the event of conflict, the Master Terms control.

1. DEFINITIONS

Event
A time-bound or scheduled activity hosted on the Platform involving live, semi-live, or asynchronous participation, including but not limited to jams, performances, showcases, workshops, tournaments, exhibitions, and virtual or mixed-reality experiences.

Jam
A collaborative creation Event in which multiple Participants contribute original content toward a shared or parallel creative objective. Jams may include, but are not limited to:

Game jams

Art or illustration jams

Music or audio jams

Writing or narrative jams

Modding or asset-creation jams

Event Organizer
A user or entity responsible for creating, configuring, publishing, administering, and moderating an Event.

Participant
Any user who contributes content, performs, collaborates, or otherwise actively participates in an Event.

Audience Member
Any user who attends, views, or experiences an Event without contributing content.

Live Event
An Event delivered in real time or near-real time, including livestreamed, VR, or synchronous digital performances.

Event Content
All content created, performed, displayed, streamed, recorded, transmitted, or distributed in connection with an Event.

Shobbl supports, subject to eligibility and policy compliance:
