SHOBBLE DEPICTED PERSON REMOVAL POLICY

Version: 1.1
Effective date: Thursday, November 26, 2026.
Last updated: November 19, 2026
Contact: team@shobble.com

1. Who can ask

1.1 If you are an actual person shown in content on Shobble, you can ask for it to be removed. On a content listing or content page, open Report / Request Removal and choose "I'm depicted in this content — Request Removal."

1.2 This is not an ordinary community report. Shobble does not hold it for the five-business-day complaint period.

2. What Shobble does

2.1 When a signed-in person asks for removal of a live listing, and the request is within the limit in section 2.3, Shobble hides that listing right away. The content record and files are kept so the review has evidence. They are not published while the case is open. The hide can be reversed. The files are not deleted automatically.

2.2 Shobble then reviews the request. The moderation record is marked urgent and is due within one business day. A reviewer can leave the content hidden, restore it, or remove it.

2.3 Shobble limits automatic protective hiding to five distinct listings for one signed-in reporter in any rolling 24 hours. A repeat request for the same listing is added to the open case and does not hide it again. A further request is still queued for urgent review and is not hidden automatically. The form says: "You've reached the limit for online takedown requests. If you need to report additional content, contact support@shobble.com for priority review." If Shobble cannot check whether that person is allowed to use automatic hiding, the request is still queued and the listing is not hidden automatically.

2.4 A request from someone who is not signed in is also queued for urgent review. It does not hide the listing by itself. Staff can still hide it.

2.5 A moderator records the request as substantiated, unsubstantiated, or abusive. An unsubstantiated or abusive request can restore the listing when that depicted-person hide is the only reason it is hidden. A separate moderation restriction, legal order, or safety hold can keep the listing hidden. Files are not deleted by that restoration. A legal preservation hold stays in place. It is not released to make the listing visible, and it does not by itself keep the listing hidden. An abusive reporter can be stopped from triggering further automatic hides. A good-faith report that is not substantiated is not, by itself, a ban. Knowingly fraudulent reporting can still lead to suspension or termination after a person reviews it.

2.6 Illegal-content reports and child-safety reports are not subject to this limit.

2.7 Email team@shobble.com if you cannot use the form. Include the page address and how to reach you. Do not send identity documents unless Shobble asks for them.

3. Appeals

3.1 The uploader or the depicted person can dispute the outcome by emailing team@shobble.com, or the uploader can use Settings → Moderation where that appeal is available.

3.2 Shobble does not currently have a designated independent neutral reviewer under contract. A dispute that Shobble and the parties do not resolve is recorded for referral to an independent neutral body, at Shobble's expense, once that body is designated. Until then, Shobble will not describe its own staff review as that independent review.

4. Records

4.1 Shobble does not publish identity documents or other private records used in this review.
