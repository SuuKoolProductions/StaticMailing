SHOBBLE COMMUNITY CONTENT GUIDELINES

Version: 1.3
Effective date: Thursday, November 26, 2026.
Application: These Guidelines apply through Shobble's designated valid acceptance process to the identified Platform participation. They replace the earlier Community Content Guidelines for that accepted scope without retrospectively rewriting acquisitions or accrued rights.

Reader notice: These Guidelines refer to sensitive themes solely to explain content boundaries. A descriptive reference does not establish that the material is hosted, permitted or eligible for earning.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble (Shobble, we), provides the Platform. These Guidelines explain how content is assessed for submission, publication, audience access and earning. They cover text, images, art, books, comics, music, podcasts, film, video, games, software, assets, interactive works and associated profiles, messages, metadata, thumbnails, previews, links and promotional material.

1.2 These Guidelines operate alongside the accepted Master Terms and Acceptable Use Policy. The selected content license and authorized Acquisition Record govern that acquisition within its own scope; the Creator Content License Agreement governs Creator authority and fulfillment. The accepted AI Framework governs contractual AI operations within underlying rights. Sexually explicit content is prohibited under Section 4. Financial, privacy and other feature instruments retain their actual subjects and parties.

1.3 These Guidelines supply no additional license, AI exception, financial deduction or data-processing consent. Mandatory law, independent component/public licenses and protected acquisition commitments remain. Publication eligibility, lawful content rights, technical access and earning permission are distinct. A license does not require Shobble to publish prohibited material; a publication restriction does not automatically cancel an acquired grant.

1.4 Shobble must provide fixed applicable versions before acceptance and retain the accepted package. Examples explain existing boundaries rather than establish hidden restrictions. These Guidelines do not revive deprecated license templates, the Music Licensing Policy or Asset Store Terms for new Offers.

2. HOW CONTENT IS ASSESSED

2.1 Check law, rights and safety. Identify the actual work and components, intended acts, relevant jurisdiction and credible rights or safety concerns. Illegal content and material prohibited by the applicable AUP or content schedule cannot be supplied through a policy exception. A platform-policy violation must not be described as a criminal offense merely because Shobble does not permit it.

2.2 Check context and presentation. Assess depiction, narrative, behavior, purpose, marketing, audience and delivery together. A disclaimer, fictional label, claimed age, censorship, payment or asserted artistic value does not override a specific prohibition. Conversely, a discussion, documentary reference, criticism, recovery account or ordinary fictional depiction is not automatically endorsement of the conduct it describes. Applicable category boundaries still govern.

2.3 Check AI activity and each affected component. Apply the exact accepted AI Use Case Policy, Schedule and published Errata. Identify the eligible actor, operation, purpose, input, output, human review and required approvals. A mixed-media work cannot conceal a prohibited component; permitted coding assistance, technical outputs, critical display and other express exceptions retain their actual boundaries. Generated material is not permitted merely because it is labeled, manually retyped or human-reviewed. Ordinary predetermined non-AI operations are not prohibited merely because they compute.

2.4 Check channel and audience. Material suitable for one audience or delivery route may be unsuitable for another. General Shobble surfaces, private messages, paid offers, and unauthenticated or public previews must satisfy the general-audience rules. Sexually explicit material is not permitted on any route. A paywall, private message, external link or hidden companion file cannot bypass a content restriction.

2.5 Check earning separately. Hosting eligibility does not authorize sales, paid memberships, commissions, tips, advertising or any other earning route. Apply the actual content, rights, financial and provider conditions for that route. An unmonetizable work cannot be included as a paid bonus, bundled benefit or nominally free subscription reward to avoid its restriction. Unrelated eligible work is assessed separately.

2.6 Record a scoped outcome. Identify the affected version, component, category, channel, territory, conditions and any required review. Routine eligible content does not require individual advance approval merely because these Guidelines describe an assessment process. Required-review categories and credible unresolved classification, rights or safety questions follow the applicable targeted review route. Approval is not legal certification, a broader grant or a guarantee of earnings.

3. GENERAL CONTENT BOUNDARIES

3.1 Do not publish unlawful or infringing material, malware, fraudulent claims, privacy violations, nonconsensual intimate imagery or content facilitating exploitation, trafficking, grooming or actual violence. Threats, targeted harassment, doxxing, hateful violence and deceptive impersonation follow the AUP. Contextual discussion remains subject to its protections and limits.

3.2 Appropriate authority must cover each offered act and component. Character, composition, recording, sample, performer, likeness, software and privacy rights are distinct. A commission payment, parody label, credit, membership or personal-use description supplies no missing permission. Valid independent licenses and applicable legal exceptions remain available; the mere presence of another person's work does not conclusively establish infringement.

3.3 Supply accurate descriptions, warnings and category tags. Consider the whole submission, including accessible alternate versions, audio, thumbnails, filenames, promotional claims and linked material. Do not remove warnings, disguise restricted themes, falsify provenance or provide an uncensored or prohibited version through a secondary route. Material changes affecting rights, classification, audience or earning must receive any required reassessment before the changed use proceeds.

3.4 A neutral reference to a sensitive topic is not automatically explicit content. Medical, educational, historical and artistic presentation must be assessed in its actual context, including the detail depicted and audience exposed. No contextual label creates an exception to prohibited imagery, child sexual abuse material, or nonconsensual intimate imagery. Use nonexplicit descriptions for ordinary reports and clarification requests.

4. SEXUALLY EXPLICIT CONTENT IS PROHIBITED

4.1 Shobble is a general-audience marketplace for human-created digital media. Sexually explicit content is prohibited on every host and in every upload type, including art, books, comics, music, audio, video, games, software, assets, messages, commissions, auctions, memberships, and previews. 
4.2 Child sexual abuse material, sexual content involving anyone under eighteen, sexualized minor-coded subjects, nonconsensual intimate imagery, sexual depictions of identifiable real people, trafficking, grooming, and sexual abuse or exploitation remain prohibited. Fiction, censorship, payment, a private message, or a claim of artistic value does not create an exception. A non-explicit report, safety discussion, or educational reference is not itself a prohibited depiction. Do not upload or resend suspected illegal imagery in order to report it.

4.3 Non-explicit romance, affection, or ordinary non-sexual nudity in an artistic context is not sexually explicit by that fact alone. It must still meet the general-audience, rights, and safety rules. A paywall, external link, or hidden companion file cannot carry sexually explicit material.

4.5 A prohibition on sexually explicit content does not cancel an unrelated lawful commission, auction, membership, game, artwork, piece of music, book, video, or license.

4.6 Classification tools can err. Human review, appeals, copyright notices, intimate-imagery removal, child-protection reports, and privacy requests remain available. 
5. CLASSIFICATION, ENFORCEMENT AND EXISTING RIGHTS

5.1 Shobble may classify, restrict, delist or reassess content for justified policy, legal, safety, security or operational reasons under accepted terms. Decisions must be objective, proportionate and limited to the actual issue, considering context and relevant rights. Neither sole-discretion wording nor a later risk label overrides accepted commitments.

5.2 A hosting decision, discovery limit, blacklist setting, earning decision, temporary access restriction and final license termination are separate outcomes. Reclassification, account closure, Creator departure, demonetization, expired verification or affiliation loss does not itself cancel permanent or unexpired acquisitions, Permanent Capacity, lawful retained copies, valid downstream grants or qualifying surviving publications.

5.3 Apply actual legal restrictions and separately assess lawful delivery, recovery, alternatives and the selected license's express remedies. No obligation requires prohibited supply or an age-gate bypass. If promised lawful continuing access cannot be restored or supplied through the agreed process, provide the applicable remedy without inventing a purchase-age cutoff. A refund's grant consequences require their own actual basis.

5.4 Shobble must provide applicable reasons, notice, cure, review and restoration under the AUP, Master Terms, selected license and relevant feature terms. Urgent legal, safety or security protection may precede notice where justified; it does not eliminate subsequent review or correction. An allegation, automated flag or unresolved payment concern alone is not final invalidation.

5.5 Automated tools may support classification only within actual authority and can err. Required human decisions, appeals, and statutory safeguards remain. Review decisions must identify their actual scope and be retrievable to the Creator. Shobble must correct unsupported decisions without undue delay. These Guidelines promise neither perfect detection nor universal prescreening.

5.6 Financial consequences follow accepted financial terms and law. A content restriction supplies no automatic earnings forfeiture, unrelated setoff or new creator indemnity. Distinguish pending earnings review, final liability and valid accrued compensation. No device inspection, remote deletion or recall of authorized local copies is granted.

6. REPORTS AND APPEALS

6.1 Report a concern through the published reporting route or legal@shobble.com. Identify the item or location, describe the concern and provide reasonably necessary lawful evidence. Do not download or resend suspected illegal imagery merely to report it. Necessary lawful files may be supplied only through an expressly authorized private review route and within its instructions.

6.2 Ordinary clarification, recovery and appeals may use support@shobble.com. Applicable complaint periods, human review, and external remedies remain. Copyright, intimate-imagery, child-protection and privacy requests retain their distinct legal procedures and deadlines. Ordinary support needs no formal litigation notice.

6.3 A good-faith question, mistaken report, unsuccessful appeal or legitimate renewed notice is not abuse merely because it concerns sensitive material or challenges Shobble. Knowingly fabricated evidence, coordinated harassment and deliberate evasion may justify scoped action under the AUP. A depicted-person request from a signed-in person can hide at most five distinct listings in 24 hours. Further requests, and requests from someone who is not signed in, still reach urgent review without another automatic hide. A moderator can restore a false hide without deleting the files. A legal preservation hold keeps those files and is not released merely because the listing is restored. An unsubstantiated good-faith report does not by itself suspend or terminate the reporter. Preserve valid statutory-notice, urgent-safety and privacy routes. Illegal-content and child-safety reports are not discarded because a depicted-person limit was reached.

6.4 Shobble must provide meaningful reasons and review subject to justified legal, security, privacy and investigation limits. Evidence may be retained only for an authorized purpose and lawful period; restricted backups and legal holds permit no unrelated use. Do not publish identity documents, private contributor histories or confidential recipient information to prove compliance.
