BRAND CONTENT ELIGIBILITY SCHEDULE

This Brand Content Eligibility Schedule (“Schedule”) is incorporated by reference into the Brand & Intellectual Property License Agreement (“Agreement”) between Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble (“Shobble”) and the undersigned Licensor. Shobble is a trading name of Shobbl Incorporated and is not a separate legal entity. References to “Shobble” mean Shobbl Incorporated. Existing contractual rights and obligations are unchanged.

Capitalized terms not defined in this Schedule have the meanings set forth in the Agreement, Shobble’s Content Classification Policy, and Shobble’s Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”).

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Schedule defines brand-specific content eligibility restrictions applicable to the Licensed IP when incorporated into Creator Content and Products made available through the Shobble Platform.

This Schedule operates in addition to, and not in place of, Shobble’s Platform-wide Content Classification Policy and AUP.

Nothing in this Schedule creates a right to publish, distribute, monetize, or access content, or limits Shobble’s discretion to classify, restrict, or remove content in accordance with its policies.

2. Platform GOVERNANCE & PRECEDENCE
2.1 Platform Policies Control

All uses of the Licensed IP are subject to:

• Shobble’s Content Classification Policy;
• Shobble’s Acceptable Use Policy; and
• Shobble’s Master Terms of Use and Incorporated Policies.

Where a conflict exists, Shobble’s Platform-wide policies control; provided, however, that this Schedule or the Agreement may impose additional restrictions on the use of the Licensed IP that are more restrictive than, but not inconsistent with, Shobble’s policies.

2.2 Brand Restrictions Are Additive Only

Licensor-specified restrictions may further limit eligible uses of the Licensed IP but may not permit content or uses that are otherwise Restricted, Blacklisted, or Forbidden under Shobble policies.

☐ 18+ (Sexualized Content; Additional Restrictions Apply - See Section 4.2)

Eligibility applies solely to the classifications selected above, except as expressly provided in Section 4.2. Content falling outside the permitted classifications is not eligible to incorporate the Licensed IP.

4. INELIGIBLE CONTENT CLASSIFICATIONS
4.1 General Prohibition

Except as expressly permitted under Section 4.2, the Licensed IP may not be incorporated into Creator Content classified as including, without limitation:

• Sexualized Content, as defined under Shobble’s Content Classification Policy;

• Sexual Violence, Exploitation, or Coercion;

• Hate, Harassment, or Extremist Content;

• Political Advocacy, Campaigning, or Ideological Promotion;

• Illegal, Fraudulent, or Deceptive Activity;

• AI-generated or AI-assisted content prohibited under Shobble’s Acceptable Use Policy or AI Use-Case Policy; or

• Any Blacklisted or Forbidden Content under Shobble policies.
4.2 Optional Restricted Content Eligibility

Notwithstanding Sections 3 and 4.1, Licensor may request eligibility for lawful, fictional 18+ content classified as Sexualized Content under Shobble’s Content Classification Policy.

Any such eligibility, if approved by Shobble, is subject at all times to the Content Classification Policy, the Acceptable Use Policy, applicable Platform requirements, and Shobble’s sole discretion. Eligibility does not imply monetization approval and may be modified or withdrawn at any time without liability.

Shobble may update its Content Classification Policy or AUP independently. Continued participation in the Program constitutes acceptance of such updates.

9. NO WAIVER

Failure to enforce this Schedule with respect to any particular content does not constitute a waiver of any rights under this Schedule or the Agreement.
