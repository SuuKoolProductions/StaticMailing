1. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Policy:

1.1 Generative AI

“Generative AI” means any system designed to generate new expressive content, including text, audio, visual, or interactive works, that substitutes for, imitates, or replicates human creative output.
This includes, without limitation, large language models (LLMs), diffusion models, generative adversarial networks (GANs), voice or likeness models, style-transfer systems, and similar technologies.

1.2 Non-Generative Machine Learning

“Non-Generative Machine Learning” means machine learning techniques used solely for analysis, optimization, simulation, classification, testing, moderation, accessibility, fraud prevention, or operational support, where such techniques do not produce expressive, creative, or substitutive outputs.

1.3 Expressive or Substitutive Output

An “Expressive or Substitutive Output” is any output that:

replaces or competes with human-authored creative works;

imitates artistic style, voice, narrative, or visual identity; or

could reasonably be consumed as creative content rather than operational output.

2. PLATFORM-LEVEL POSITION

Shobble is a human-authored, compliance-first platform.

The Platform is designed to support lawful creative expression, distribution, and monetization without enabling the extraction, replication, or replacement of creator labor through generative systems.

As such:

Generative AI use of Platform Content is categorically disfavored and prohibited by default.

Non-Generative Machine Learning is permitted only in narrow, expressly authorized contexts.

3. PROHIBITED AI USES (ABSOLUTE)

Unless expressly authorized through a separate, written agreement executed by Shobble, the following uses are strictly prohibited:

3.1 Generative AI Training and Operation

Using any Content to:

train, fine-tune, validate, evaluate, or benchmark Generative AI systems;

operate Generative AI systems that produce expressive or substitutive outputs;

generate synthetic text, images, audio, music, dialogue, assets, or interactive content.

3.2 Dataset Creation and Model Inputs

Extracting, aggregating, or compiling Content (including assets, metadata, stems, or derivatives) into datasets for AI or machine learning use;

Creating embeddings, weights, feature vectors, or latent representations derived from Content for generative or substitutive purposes.

3.3 Style, Voice, and Likeness Modeling

Training or operating systems intended to imitate artistic style, narrative voice, musical performance, vocal characteristics, or creative identity;

Voice cloning, likeness synthesis, or identity modeling based on Platform Content.

3.4 Circumvention and Shadow Use

Using Content indirectly, through intermediaries or proxies, to evade this Policy;

Claiming public availability, Creative Commons designation, or technical accessibility as justification for prohibited AI use.

4. CONDITIONAL USE: NON-GENERATIVE MACHINE LEARNING
4.1 Permitted in Principle

Non-Generative Machine Learning may be permitted where all of the following are true:

the use does not produce expressive, creative, or substitutive outputs;

the use does not involve dataset creation or reuse beyond the authorized scope;

the use is expressly authorized by the applicable license, Platform feature, or written approval.

Examples of potentially permitted uses (subject to authorization):

physics simulation and pathfinding;

performance optimization and testing;

quality assurance and bug detection;

accessibility tooling (e.g., tagging, classification);

moderation, safety, fraud detection, or anti-cheat systems;

compression, upscaling, or denoising that does not alter creative expression.

4.2 No Implied Permission

The absence of Generative AI output does not by itself authorize machine learning use.

If a use case is not clearly authorized, it is prohibited.

5. CREATOR CONTENT & LICENSE INTERACTION
5.1 No Implied AI License

No license—whether proprietary, Creative Commons, educational, royalty-free, or otherwise—grants any right to use Content for Generative AI or prohibited ML purposes unless explicitly stated in a separate agreement.

Public licensing selections do not override this Policy.

5.2 Creator Protection

This Policy exists to protect:

creator livelihoods and market value;

originality and authorship;

licensing integrity across games, assets, music, and mixed media.

Unauthorized AI use may constitute both contractual breach and intellectual property infringement.

6. SHOBBLE INTERNAL USE

Unless explicitly disclosed and consented to by the Creator through a separate agreement, Shobble itself does not use Creator Content for:

Generative AI training or operation;

dataset creation for AI systems;

style, voice, or content modeling.

This restriction applies regardless of Content visibility or access level.

7. ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this Policy may result in:

immediate suspension or termination of access;

removal or restriction of Content;

revocation of licenses or permissions;

legal action, including injunctive relief and damages.

Unauthorized Generative AI use is deemed knowing and intentional where this Policy applies.

8. NO WAIVER; RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

Public access, discoverability, or technical accessibility of Content does not constitute consent to AI or machine learning use.

All rights not expressly granted are reserved by Creators and by Shobbl.

9. LICENSING & EXCEPTIONS

Requests for AI training, dataset use, or other non-standard machine learning use must be submitted through channels expressly designated by Shobble and require a separate, written agreement.

Silence or inaction does not constitute consent.
