SHOBBLE ACCOUNT ELIGIBILITY & ACCESS POLICY

Version: 1.2
Effective date: the date this version is first published on shobble.com. Adopted in source on November 19, 2026. This version does not take effect merely by existing in source control, and it does not apply to conduct before that publication. Until publication, Version 1.1 (effective November 16, 2026) remains the version shown to users. That prior version is preserved at Git tag archive/adult-capable-2026-11-19.
Application: This Policy applies through Shobble's designated valid acceptance process to the identified Platform participation. It replaces the earlier Account Eligibility & Access Policy for that accepted scope without retrospectively rewriting acquisitions, recipient grants or accrued rights.

1. PURPOSE AND DOCUMENT RELATIONSHIPS

1.1 Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble (Shobble, we), provides the Platform. This Policy governs account and feature eligibility, age and regional restrictions, verification, correction and recovery. Content eligibility follows the accepted AUP, Community Content Guidelines and applicable content schedules; legal capacity, payment-provider eligibility and content permissions are separate matters.

1.2 The Master Terms govern remaining Platform matters and subject-specific priority. The selected Personal Use, Commercial Use, Enterprise or Educational license and authorized Acquisition Record govern only that acquisition. The Delegated Access & Managed Accounts Policy governs supported administration within actual grants. AI, financial, privacy and processing instruments retain their actual subjects, parties and requirements. No instrument authorizes sexually explicit content.

1.3 This Policy creates no new content license, management authority, processing consent or financial deduction. Mandatory law, independent component/public licenses and protected acquisition commitments remain. Account access, current credentials, content grants, Capacity and surviving recipient rights are distinct. A restriction on one does not automatically cancel the others.

1.4 Shobble must disclose material age, regional, verification and feature limits before the affected registration, activation or acquisition and retain the applicable fixed terms. It must not knowingly accept an Offer for a person ineligible to receive it without a lawful supported arrangement. An internal setting, later receipt or unidentified policy cannot add undisclosed contractual restrictions.

2. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY AND AUTHORITY

2.1 General Shobble use requires age sixteen or older, subject to higher local ages, regional prohibitions and other lawful disclosed eligibility requirements. Sixteen is the minimum supported age, not a universal statement of contractual majority or eligibility for every feature.

2.2 Provide accurate required age, location, contact and eligibility information, keep material details current, and use only accounts and features you are authorized to access. Public pseudonyms need not disclose your legal name, but required verification, contracting, payment and tax information must be accurate. Do not impersonate a participant or submit false evidence.

2.3 A person acting for another individual, an institution or an organization must have actual authority for the act. Payment, an email domain, employment, enrollment, parent status or being the first administrator does not alone establish the Holder or authority over every account, purchase or private file.

2.4 Feature eligibility may differ for purchasing, recurring billing, creator monetization, payouts, commissions, advertising, events, API access or managed administration. Disclose and apply the actual legal-capacity, age, provider, rights and verification conditions before activation. General account eligibility does not imply that a user can enter every contract or receive payouts.

2.5 No optional feature, payment method, supported region or management route is promised merely because it is mentioned here. Technical availability does not supply missing eligibility, authority or acceptance. Actual promised service, acquisition and remedy commitments remain.

3. USERS BELOW LEGAL MAJORITY AND MANAGED ACCESS

3.1 Users who meet the applicable minimum age but remain below legal majority must have legally required parental or guardian permission and applicable notices/privacy safeguards. Independent account administration and legal capacity are different. A required guardian permission must cover the actual activity; a general checkbox cannot replace it.

3.2 There is no supported under-sixteen account exception through a parent, guardian, school, homeschooling, gift, employer, educational license or Managed Account. A higher applicable local restriction cannot be bypassed through management. Public nonexplicit policy, reporting, privacy and support routes remain available without requiring an ordinary account.

3.3 Supported management requires appropriate authority and the actual personal, educational or organizational permission. A teacher is not automatically a parent or authorized institutional purchaser. Managers may not create unauthorized accounts, claim another person's assent, share credentials or expose users to content or features for which they are ineligible.

3.4 Apply actual age-appropriate defaults and feature safeguards and disclose material administrative visibility. Management does not authorize unrestricted access to private communications, personal acquisitions or unrelated activity. Mandatory personal-data rights and the participant's actual authority remain.

3.5 Reaching majority, graduating or leaving management does not automatically transfer an acquisition, create a new license, trigger an undisclosed grace period or require repurchase. Distinguish temporary delegated access, institution-held participation, authorized surviving sublicenses and direct recipient acquisitions. Their continuation and evidence follow their actual grants and the Managed Accounts Policy.

4. NO ADULT CONTENT SERVICE

4.1 Shobble does not offer a separate adult account, adult verification for sexual content, or adult purchases. Age sixteen remains the general minimum, and higher ages still apply where a feature or region requires them.

4.2 A payment card, declared birth date, subscription, gift, or delegated assignment does not create access to sexually explicit content. Each person must still meet the age rule for the feature they actually use.

4.3 There is no adult blacklist and no adult subdomain. Do not use shared credentials, links, previews, or APIs to seek a bypass.

4.4 Enterprise and Educational licenses do not add an adult catalog. Existing lawful acquisitions keep their accepted terms.

4.5 Policy information, reporting, privacy requests, ordinary support, and appeals do not require a special adult account. Use the published reporting routes rather than uploading suspect sexual material to make a report.

5. VERIFICATION AND PERSONAL INFORMATION

5.1 Shobble may require proportionate verification for actual age, identity, region, representative-authority, recovery, payment, legal or credible fraud/security needs. Checks must be suited to the actual requirement and use the least intrusive effective lawful method, with an appropriate alternative where required. Reverification needs an identified continuing or new basis; an expired result alone is not final invalidation of an acquisition.

5.2 Verification documents, selfies and liveness inputs use the designated authorized provider or secure collection route, not ordinary support, public profiles or an Acquisition Record. Shobble receives only necessary disclosed results and compliance attributes that law permits it to receive and retain. Tell users the actual provider role, information, purpose, material consequences and relevant privacy notice before collection.

5.3 The Privacy Policy and applicable processing/transfer instruments govern legal bases, sensitive or biometric conditions, recipients, countries, retention and rights. Policy acceptance, a managed role or verification is not blanket consent to profiling, advertising, model development or provider improvement. Pseudonymous results do not escape stricter local collection, identification or deletion limits.

5.4 Authorized automated tools may assist but can err. A signal, inconsistent record or unsuccessful attempt is not conclusive proof of age, fraud or deliberate evasion. Shobble must provide the explanations, correction and human review required by law or promised in the accepted instruments. No general permission for a solely automated legally or similarly significant decision is created here.

6. REGIONAL AVAILABILITY AND TRAVEL

6.1 Availability may differ by account role, product, content category, payment or payout route and current location. Actual law, sanctions/export controls, provider scope and disclosed operational limits determine supported activity. A privacy notice discussing a jurisdiction does not open service there.

6.2 Do not deliberately evade applicable regional restrictions or falsify location/eligibility information. VPNs, proxies and privacy tools are not prohibited merely by their use, but cannot be used to bypass an actual restriction. A network or IP signal alone may be inaccurate and must have a correction route.

6.3 Shobble must identify material restrictions before the affected purchase or activation and apply lawful controls. A new transaction may be unavailable while an existing acquisition retains its accepted scope. Travel, relocation or a later service restriction requires a separate assessment of lawful delivery, recovery and remedies rather than automatic worldwide cancellation.

6.4 Planned material regional changes require the applicable notice and transition process; urgent legal or security restrictions may precede notice where justified. Shobble must not promise access contrary to binding law, but must assess lawful alternatives and preserve unaffected rights. Provider restrictions create no undisclosed customer liability or blanket cancellation power.

7. CORRECTION, RECOVERY AND REPRESENTATION

7.1 Users may request correction of mistaken age, region, affiliation, authority or account status through supported settings or support@shobble.com. Shobble will explain reasonably necessary evidence, provide proportionate verification and reassess erroneous restrictions. Do not require unnecessary raw identity documents through ordinary email.

7.2 Where management or another account configuration is legally required and supported, establish the necessary authority, notices and effective permissions before assigning a Manager. Shobble must not appoint a stranger or give an institution control of private accounts merely because of an email domain or disputed age signal. Restrict affected activity where necessary while a lawful arrangement is resolved.

7.3 Same-Holder contact/name corrections, administrator replacement, device replacement and recovery do not themselves transfer the acquisition or require repurchase merely to restore a valid grant. An actual transfer to another legal Holder needs the governing permission, required consents or independent legal basis. Verification of ownership and management authority must protect against account takeover.

7.4 Loss of a school/employer login, graduation, staff turnover, family-management change or identity-provider failure does not itself cancel an effectively issued independent or surviving recipient grant. Supply the reasonable access and privacy-preserving evidence recovery promised by the selected license and Managed Accounts Policy. Missing paperwork calls for investigation and correction, not automatic forfeiture.

8. RESTRICTIONS, CONTINUITY AND REVIEW

8.1 Credible eligibility, legal, fraud or security concerns may justify proportionate interim limits on the affected account or feature. Explain material facts, basis, scope, duration or release conditions and review route where lawful. An unresolved check or allegation alone does not authorize indefinite restriction, automatic earnings forfeiture or a finding of misconduct.

8.2 Curable material breaches receive the notice and reasonable cure opportunity required by the Master Terms and selected license. Immediate protection is permitted where required by law or reasonably necessary against serious harm that cannot await cure, with required subsequent notice, review and correction. Deliberate falsification may justify a distinct sanction; innocent error and underlying ineligibility are not interchangeable.

8.3 Account suspension, reclassification, closure, expired verification, a regional change or loss of affiliation does not itself cancel permanent or unexpired content rights, Permanent Capacity, valid downstream grants, qualifying surviving outputs or earned compensation. Apply actual grounds and scope to any final rights decision rather than treating credentials as the grant.

8.4 Shobble must separately assess lawful continuing library access, recovery and usable alternatives under the selected license. No prohibited supply or age-gate bypass is required. If the promised lawful continuing route cannot be restored or supplied through the agreed process, provide its express remedy or another remedy the affected holder validly agrees to, without an invented purchase-age cutoff. Mandatory remedies remain.

8.5 On a lawful end of participation, stop only affected access and follow actual cancellation, private-file retrieval, available export, retention and final-payment duties. Closure alone supplies no remote-deletion authority or general recall of authorized lawful copies. Refund accounting and rescission of a grant are distinct; a representative cannot surrender another person's independent rights without authority.

8.6 Affected users may seek review through support@shobble.com or the applicable published route. Shobble must consider actual evidence, relevant licenses and asserted legal rights, give required reasons and human review, reassess interim measures and promptly correct unsupported decisions. Apply any more specific financial or statutory periods and external remedies; ordinary support requires no formal litigation notice. Good-faith questions, mistakes and unsuccessful appeals are not abuse merely because they challenge a decision.

9. CHANGES AND CONTACT

9.1 Changes follow the Master Terms' actual notice, effective-date and acceptance process, ordinarily including at least thirty days' advance notice of material adverse changes unless another lawful period or urgent legal/security requirement applies. Keep accepted versions retrievable. Publication or continued use alone does not replace required assent.

9.2 Changes apply within their valid prospective scope without silently rewriting acquisitions, recipient grants, accepted AI sets or accrued financial duties. Current mandatory law and justified urgent protection retain their actual effect. Refusal of optional successor terms alone does not forfeit existing rights or remove valid remedies.

Eligibility, verification correction, recovery, managed access, privacy and appeals: support@shobble.com
Legal concerns and reports: legal@shobble.com

If a contact route changes, Shobble will provide a reasonably accessible successor. Applicable statutory-notice procedures remain. Ordinary support, reporting and privacy requests need no separate adult account.
