Application: This Policy applies through Shobble's designated valid acceptance process to the identified Platform participation. It replaces the earlier Acceptable Use Policy for that accepted scope without retrospectively rewriting acquisitions, accrued earnings or independent rights.

1. PURPOSE, SCOPE AND DOCUMENT RELATIONSHIPS

1.1 Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble (Shobble, we), provides the Platform. This Policy governs acceptable conduct and use of its accounts, submissions, listings, communications, marketplaces, delivery routes, APIs and other enabled features. It applies to users, Creators, representatives and partners within their actual participation and authority.

1.2 The accepted Master Terms govern remaining Platform matters and subject-specific document priority. The selected Personal Use, Commercial Use, Enterprise or Educational license and authorized Acquisition Record govern that acquisition within its own scope. The Creator Content License Agreement governs Creator authority, approved Offers and surviving fulfillment. The accepted AI Use Case Policy, Schedule and published Errata (AI Framework) govern contractual AI activity within underlying rights. Financial and other feature instruments govern their actual subjects and parties when validly accepted. This Policy creates no additional content license, financial deduction, processing consent or general agency. It does not authorize sexually explicit content.

1.3 Mandatory law, independently applicable component/public licenses and the protected acquisition baseline remain. No general priority statement, classification or enforcement discretion makes acquired grants revocable at will, supplies missing third-party rights, or removes promised continuity, independently lawful-use protections, review or remedies. Permission under one instrument does not establish publication, earning, privacy or provider authority under another.

1.4 Shobble must make the exact applicable Policy and incorporated rules accessible before acceptance and retain retrievable accepted versions. Community Content Guidelines explain classifications and examples; they do not supply undisclosed restrictions or override accepted rights. A later live page, internal work order or receipt cannot manufacture an unaccepted term.

2. UNLAWFUL AND HARMFUL CONDUCT

2.1 Do not use the Platform for unlawful activity, fraud, theft, trafficking, exploitation, money laundering, sanctions or export-control evasion, or unauthorized regulated goods or services. Applicable law means law actually applicable to the person, activity or service; this Policy does not require every submission to comply with every jurisdiction worldwide.

2.2 Do not threaten, stalk, harass, intimidate or coerce others; solicit or facilitate violence or self-harm; promote hateful violence or targeted dehumanizing abuse based on protected characteristics; or unlawfully discriminate. Do not disclose private identifying, intimate, financial or confidential information without an appropriate legal basis or use it to expose someone to harm.

2.3 Do not groom, sexually solicit or exploit minors, distribute child sexual abuse material, facilitate sexual trafficking, or publish nonconsensual intimate imagery or covered digital forgeries. Do not solicit, commission, link to or conceal prohibited material to evade these rules. Actual threats, exploitation and unlawful material remain prohibited regardless of fictional framing or disclaimers. Shobble does not condone human sex trafficking. Shobble will report every suspected instance of human sex trafficking to the appropriate authorities.

2.4 Assess the conduct and its context. Depiction, criticism, reporting, education, recovery discussion, or discussion of wrongdoing is not automatically endorsement or facilitation. It must still satisfy applicable content, audience, and rights restrictions. Context does not excuse an actual threat, targeted abuse, child sexual abuse material, or a prohibited sexual depiction. Sexually explicit content is prohibited on every host.

3. CONTENT, RIGHTS AND CLASSIFICATION

3.1 Do not upload, offer or distribute unlawful or infringing material, malware, fraudulent claims or content violating applicable privacy, publicity, confidentiality or data-protection rights. Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent authorship, identity, authority, rights, eligibility or an endorsement. Clearly identified lawful parody, criticism and pseudonymous participation are not impersonation merely because they refer to another person.

3.2 Hold the rights or another supportable legal basis for each offered act and component. Owning a file, paying for a commission, crediting a source, applying a parody label or obtaining one component's permission supplies no missing rights. Conversely, a valid independent license or applicable legal exception need not originate on Shobble. A claim, notice or automated match alone does not conclusively establish infringement. Copyright notices and counter-notices follow the published DMCA procedure and law.

3.3 Use the correct audience, category, warnings and material disclosures, including for previews, alternate tracks, companion files, links and later edits. Do not hide a restricted component in an otherwise eligible work. Publication, discovery, advertising, sales and continuing delivery are separate decisions. Approval is limited to its actual reviewed scope and is not legal certification or a promise of earnings.

3.4 Sexually explicit content is prohibited on every host and upload type. Review of a safety report does not authorize publication of sexually explicit material.

3.5 Where more than one safety rule applies, the rule that prohibits the material governs. A later label cannot turn prohibited sexual content into a lawful upload or extinguish an unrelated acquired grant. Later restrictions on other content require the distinct rights and remedy assessment in Section 9.

4. PLATFORM INTEGRITY, COLLECTION AND SECURITY

4.1 Do not disrupt or overload systems, introduce malicious code, steal credentials, access accounts or private material without authority, defeat applicable access controls, or use unauthorized bots, distributed requests or intermediaries to evade restrictions. API access and automation must remain within the authorized feature, data, purpose and rate limits.

4.2 Do not scrape, harvest, extract or systematically collect content or user data without actual authorization, subject to independently applicable rights and the accepted licenses' lawful-use protections. Public availability, a purchase, an absent barrier or a robots allowance is not a collection license. Do not neutralize protective transformations to enable prohibited collection, training or reuse.

4.3 Authorized ordinary capture, permitted excerpts and gameplay, licensed production copies, supported accessibility, and other actually granted or independently protected uses are not prohibited merely because they use recording, copying or automation. Their scope does not authorize unrelated bulk collection, source redistribution, privacy violations or circumvention. Access-control and circumvention rules have their own applicable legal requirements.

4.4 Do not probe, scan, exploit or otherwise test Shobble's systems outside an expressly authorized security scope or independently applicable legal right. Reverse engineering, decompilation and disassembly restrictions apply only to the extent lawfully imposed and do not negate an acquired permission or protected interoperability, repair or security-research right. No public testing authorization or immunity is created here. Report a suspected vulnerability privately without unnecessarily accessing, retaining or disclosing others' data.

5. FRAUD, ENGAGEMENT AND PROCESS ABUSE

5.1 Do not use stolen payment methods, fabricate transactions, manipulate auctions or payouts, conceal material fees, falsify rights or verification evidence, or engage in intentional refund or chargeback fraud. A good-faith dispute, withdrawal request, mistaken payment or unresolved chargeback is not fraud merely because it reduces revenue.

5.2 Do not buy or generate fake engagement, use coordinated accounts or automation to inflate allocations, collude on sham commission bids, or misrepresent qualifying activity. For the Creator Fund, the accepted Schedule determines qualifying events, follow or membership gates, eligibility, weighting and allocation. Genuine permitted repeat activity, a paid membership's disclosed weighting or an ordinary qualifying auction bid is not manipulation merely because it affects a score.

5.3 Do not knowingly fabricate allegations or evidence, coordinate abusive reporting, impersonate a complainant or evade a valid restriction through replacement accounts or disguised submissions. Good-faith questions, unsuccessful reports, appeals and renewed notices with material new grounds are not abuse. Patterns may support a reasoned decision but must be assessed individually; common ownership, disagreement or an adverse outcome alone is insufficient.

6. MARKETPLACE, MONETIZATION AND FINANCIAL SAFEGUARDS

6.1 Creators must provide accurate necessary identity, rights, tax and payout information; authorize complete Offers; disclose material product, price, access, license and dependency terms before acquisition; and fulfill accepted obligations. A selected license or completed payment does not make an otherwise ineligible work publishable or monetizable.

6.2 Fees, royalties, refunds, payout conditions, reserves, reversals and recovery follow the actual accepted Creator Monetization Terms and applicable feature schedule. This Policy imposes no additional percentage, payout forfeiture, indemnity or automatic offset. Private provider arrangements cannot create undisclosed user liabilities.

6.3 A credible financial or rights concern may justify an identified proportionate interim hold under an accepted or legal basis. Shobble must distinguish pending review from final liability, explain and reassess the measure where lawful, provide correction/review, and release amounts when its basis ends. Demonetization, account closure or unpaid status alone does not make earned compensation unearned.

6.4 Creator Fund allocations, subscriber carry, costs, finalization, payout timing, privacy and exit follow its accepted Schedule. General fraud or enforcement language does not transfer Shobble's assigned ordinary losses to innocent Creators, round away earned fractions, treat unallocated subscriber support as Creator proceeds, or authorize confiscation. A subscriber's own allocation report does not authorize disclosure of individual contributors to Creators.

7. AI AND AUTOMATION

7.1 Every AI operation and affected component must satisfy the exact applicable AI Framework's actor, purpose, inputs, output, boundaries, Outcome, human review, approvals and provider conditions. Prohibited, Pending and unavailable activities cannot proceed outside an actual express exception. Conditional activities require verified completion of their conditions. A generic written permission, paid tier or similar third-party tool does not bypass them.

7.2 Do not submit or monetize material produced or modified through an unauthorized AI activity unless an express Framework permission covers that operation and intended submission. Preserve the Framework's bounded coding assistance, critical display, technical outputs, internal placeholders and other express exceptions within their actual limits. Ordinary predetermined non-AI computation is not prohibited merely because it computes. Disclosure, human review, tracing, retyping or performance does not cure prohibited generation.

7.3 Do not use entrusted content or user data for dataset preparation, training, fine-tuning, evaluation, benchmarking, model improvement, style or voice modeling, or independent provider reuse without the Framework's actual purpose-limited permission and all required rights, agreements, approvals and privacy authority. Operational moderation, verification, search or recommendation permission does not establish model-development permission. Acceptance, uploading or purchase alone is not training consent.

7.4 Preserve required provenance, edit history, notices and human review. Do not conceal material AI involvement or falsely claim human authorship. Provider access, artifacts, retention and deletion must remain purpose-limited under the accepted Framework and applicable processing instruments. Shobble-only permissions are not user permissions; conditional proprietary-feature permissions require the exact approved release and scope. Internal integration records create no permission.

7.5 Sexually explicit content, including a sexual voice performance or sexual voice cloning, is prohibited on every host. This Policy does not silently amend a buyer acquisition's accepted AI set or authorize a new processing purpose for previously entrusted files. Non-sexual AI activity remains governed by the AI Framework.

8.2 Account age rules in Section 8.1 still apply. Public policy information, reporting, support, and privacy requests stay available. Do not evade age, regional, or account controls. A VPN or privacy tool is not prohibited merely by its use.

8.3 Sexually explicit material is prohibited on general surfaces, paid surfaces, private messages, and public or unauthenticated previews. Child sexual abuse material, sexualized minor-coded subjects, nonconsensual intimate imagery, sexual depictions of identifiable real people, and sexual abuse or exploitation remain prohibited. Fiction, censorship, free hosting, or a paywall does not override a prohibition.

8.4 Creator monetization of lawful general-audience work follows the accepted monetization terms. A creator fund does not include sexually explicit content.

8.5 Use supported delegation rather than sharing credentials. Apply the selected license's actual holder, participant, device, seat or other metric; do not presume one active user always consumes one separate license. Administrator status, employment, graduation or affiliation changes do not themselves cancel organization-held acquisitions, valid independent learner grants or surviving Client/portfolio rights. Recovery and reassignment follow the actual accepted scope.

9. ENFORCEMENT, ACQUIRED RIGHTS AND CONTINUITY

9.1 Shobble may investigate credible concerns and impose justified warnings, content or visibility restrictions, affected-feature limits, monetization suspension, API revocation or account action under accepted terms and law. Decisions must be objective, proportionate and scoped to the actual issue, considering context, severity, recurrence, apparent intent and harm. Automated flags may assist but do not conclusively establish illegality, breach or final financial liability.

9.2 Before terminating acquired rights for curable material breach, identify the conduct, affected rights, required correction and reasonable cure opportunity under the selected license and Master Terms. Immediate measures may precede notice where required by law or reasonably necessary against serious safety/security harm, fraud, deliberate unauthorized distribution or a risk that cannot await cure. Urgency does not remove required subsequent reasons, review or correction. Ordinary prospective service exit and final license termination are distinct.

9.3 Shobble will give required adverse-decision reasons identifying material facts, the rule or legal basis, affected scope, duration or release conditions where known, material automation and available review. Notices must be sufficiently meaningful to challenge the actual decision, subject to justified legal, security, privacy and investigation limits. Required durable-medium notices, business-user termination periods and other statutory safeguards remain.

9.4 Delisting, reclassification, demonetization, account or API suspension, Creator departure, affiliation loss or a pending payment/verification issue does not itself revoke permanent or unexpired content rights, Permanent Capacity, valid downstream grants or qualifying surviving publications. Shobble must separately assess lawful continuing delivery, recovery, alternatives and the selected license's express remedies. No prohibited supply or access bypass is required. Where the promised continuing route cannot lawfully be restored or supplied, apply the accepted remedy without an invented purchase-age cutoff.

9.5 Interim measures must be reassessed and lifted when their basis ends. Following an overturned decision, correct records and promptly restore lawful access, visibility, eligibility and valid amounts as applicable. Necessary restrictions on one work, feature or account do not justify unrelated purchase or earnings forfeiture. This Policy grants no invasive device inspection, remote deletion or recall of authorized local copies.

10.1 Report suspected violations through the published reporting tools or legal@shobble.com; ordinary questions and appeals may use support@shobble.com. Submit necessary lawful review files only through an expressly authorized private route and within its instructions. 10.2 Shobble will apply the required notice-and-action, copyright, intimate-imagery, child-protection, threat-reporting and evidence-preservation processes under the Master Terms and applicable law. Statutory notices retain their actual requirements; an ordinary policy report need not satisfy a separate statutory form to receive appropriate safety review. A notice alone is not a court determination, and an appeal cannot require unlawful restoration.

10.3 Affected users and reporters may request review through the applicable published route, including support@shobble.com. Shobble will consider actual permissions, relevant context, asserted exceptions and correction evidence, provide required human review and a reasoned outcome, and correct unsupported decisions without undue delay. Applicable statutory complaint periods and external remedies remain. This Policy does not shorten a specific feature's review or payment deadline or require formal litigation notice for ordinary support.

10.4 For repeated manifest abuse of reporting or complaints, apply any required prior warning, identify the conduct and correction, and use a proportionate restriction with reasons, duration and review. Preserve routes for valid statutory notices, urgent safety reports and privacy requests. Unsuccessful good-faith challenges do not justify punishment.

10.5 Cooperate with lawful, proportionate requests within actual authority and applicable privacy/confidentiality limits. This is not consent to unrestricted audits, device searches, disclosure of confidential recipient data or surrender of legal defenses. Shobble and its providers may retain necessary evidence only for an authorized purpose and lawful period; restricted backups and legal holds permit no unrelated reuse.
