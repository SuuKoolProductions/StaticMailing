1. Application, parties and scope

1.1 This Schedule, including Appendix A, is between Shobbl Incorporated, doing business as Shobble, and each identified subscriber or participating Creator who actually accepts it for that role. A representative must have authority. Subscriber provisions govern funding, activity, carry, return and subscriber information; Creator provisions govern participation, compensation and Creator information. Acceptance by one role does not bind another person or create a contract between every subscriber and Creator.

1.2 The accepted package must identify this version, its effective participation period, supported products, territories, content/formats, and actual payment conditions. Creator enrollment must identify the authorized legal payout entity and destination. This Schedule supplements the accepted Creator Monetization Terms for Creators and the identified platform-subscription terms for subscribers; accepted Master Terms govern remaining platform matters. It replaces earlier Fund schedules and separate calculation tables only for the scope validly accepted, preserving prior accrued rights. Publication alone makes no amendment.

1.3 This program uses USD monthly platform subscriptions, USD Creator payouts and eligible general-platform content. Afterdark earnings, annual plans, app-store purchases, other currencies, platform trials, gifts and credits require separately identified and accepted treatment before inclusion. A gifted creator membership under Section 3 is distinct from gifting the platform subscription that funds the budget. No minimum income or launch of an unsupported feature is promised.

1.4 The subscriber purchases the platform services identified in the subscription offer. Shobble commits the designated share of eligible subscription revenue to creator support: through qualifying engagement while the account is open or after timely eligible reopening, and through the disclosed creator-contest fallback where Section 4 applies. The Fund is separate from Creator Sales, creator membership charges, advertising, commissions and tips. No 5%, 8%, 15% or additional Fund platform fee applies to Fund compensation. Participation supplies no new content license, ownership, exclusivity, general agency, AI permission or royalty clearance. Actual rights, royalties and surviving fulfillment follow the Creator Agreement, selected license and necessary independent authority. Shobble must obtain required actual approvals and perform this commitment; internal implementation cannot reduce it.

2. Funding and allocation

2.1 Eligible Price is the actual discounted price attributable to supported paid platform-subscription service, excluding tax collected for remittance and separately charged lawful payment fees. Ordinary processing and payout costs do not reduce the price or Fund share. Shobble bears those costs and its own income tax from its resources. A subsidy increases the Fund base only if expressly identified and funded; an unpaid charge or grace period supplies no new budget.

2.2 Any allocation of a bundled price among subscription service and other benefits must reflect the genuine offer, use a reasonable consistent method, and be disclosed before affected Creator participation and customer purchase. No nominal allocation, internal relabeling or later accounting change may retrospectively reduce the accepted Fund base or count the same price twice.

2.3 A calendar month runs from 00:00 UTC on its first day to, excluding, the next month’s start. For each paid interval, Month Service Price = Eligible Price × the actual service duration within the month ÷ the interval’s total service duration. Use nonoverlapping coverage. The new subscriber Fund budget is 50% of those service prices. Cancellation stops future funding as applicable, not qualifying activity during paid service or existing-carry allocation. Late settlement must receive an attributable historical adjustment, not be mislabeled zero engagement.

2.4 Available Budget = opening unallocated subscriber carry + new Fund budget − valid attributable unallocated-budget adjustments. Event Points = Appendix A base points × the event-time multiplier in Section 3. Creator Score is the eligible weighted total after caps and higher-of rules. Total Score is the sum of eligible Creator Scores for that subscriber and period. At the allocation cutoff, if Total Score is positive, Creator Share = Available Budget × Creator Score ÷ Total Score; if zero, the Available Budget carries under Section 4. Scores reset each period. Any positive qualifying activity can allocate the entire Available Budget, including accumulated carry, even if it is only one event or benefits one Creator. Disclose that outcome with the subscriber’s Fund information. Points are relative weights, not dollars or fixed payments per action; 2× weighting does not guarantee twice the payment.

2.5 For example, a $20 interval from November 25 at 00:00 UTC to December 25 at 00:00 UTC has six November days and 24 December days: $4 and $16 of service price, generating $2 and $8 of new Fund budget. Genuine zero engagement carries the relevant budget. January and February zero-score budgets of $10 each, followed by March’s $10 budget and positive scores, make $30 available in March; final score shares of 40%, 20%, 20%, 20% allocate $12, $6, $6, $6.

3. Eligible activity and membership weighting

3.1 An event qualifies only where: the subscriber has funded platform service or existing unallocated carry; follows or has an active membership/subscription to the earning Creator at the event time; and the Creator/payout entity is enrolled and approved for this earning route and the work/auction is eligible. The event must satisfy Appendix A and Section 5. A platform subscription alone is not the creator relationship. Free activity with no funding or carry creates no budget. A subscriber whose eligible allocation window is funded by carry remains eligible to generate otherwise qualifying scores through its cutoff; provisional allocations or accounting reclassifications do not consume the carry early or disqualify later activity in that window. Actual lawful holds, exhausted funding and account/participation cutoffs retain their scope. General account age or verification does not alone establish contractual, bidding or payment eligibility.

3.2 Shobble must disclose material earning exclusions and supported formats and enable subscribers to identify the applicable relationship gate, earning status and multiplier. Hosting permission and pending approval alone do not qualify. Later following, approval or membership does not retrospectively qualify activity unless an expressly authorized, funded promotion provides otherwise. Ordinary subsequent unfollowing, membership expiry, removal or Creator exit preserves historical qualifying scores; attributable legal, rights, fraud or error corrections require the basis and review in Section 8.

3.3 Use 2× for an event covered by an active genuinely paid membership to its earning Creator; otherwise 1× if the relationship gate is met. Paid discounted memberships and genuine paid gifts qualify; the registered gift recipient, not also the donor, receives the benefit. Shobble-funded memberships qualify only where actually funded for the identified period. Free, complimentary, fully discounted unfunded tiers and unpaid trials receive no bonus. Tier price, multiple memberships and following as well cannot stack above 2×.

3.4 Apply membership status to each event or affected playback second. Ordinary cancellation preserves 2× through the paid entitlement period; failed renewal ends it thereafter. A pending payment without confirmed funding makes the bonus estimate provisional, not fraudulent. An ordinary later refund/reversal does not alone remove an innocent user’s valid historical multiplier. Established fabricated, unauthorized or sham entitlement may be corrected after review. Later membership never upgrades earlier events or resets caps. For books/comics, weight each page/download event first, then use the higher of the weighted page total and weighted download award per work/month, not their sum.

3.5 Each work identifies one authorized legal payout entity. Co-creators manage their own valid splits unless Shobble separately enables accepted contributor terms; one person cannot redirect another’s earnings without authority. New participation scores only from its disclosed start under the common version. Artificial activity, sham self-dealing and fabricated demand do not qualify; accessibility use, legitimate repetition, a shared network or a family relationship alone does not establish abuse.

4. Subscriber carry and final disposition

4.1 Carry is the unallocated amount of Shobble’s contractual creator-support commitment measured by reference to a subscriber’s eligible subscription activity. It is not a separately purchased subscriber cash balance, deposit, loan, transferable credit or promise to return subscription principal. Dollar-denominated records measure the support still to be allocated; they do not by themselves establish that particular money is owned by the subscriber or an unidentified Creator. The subscriber may enforce the promised calculation, allocation and restoration and any applicable remedy. Shobble must apply the designated share to the agreed creator-support purposes, not cancel it or treat it as available for unrelated expenditure merely because engagement is zero. Only the 50% share carries, without ordinary open-account expiry or dormancy fee. Closure follows the one-year restoration opportunity and contest fallback below; mandatory law retains its effect.

4.2 A cancelled, expired or nonrenewed subscriber may allocate existing carry through otherwise qualifying activity on lawfully accessible content without renewing the platform subscription. Carry creates no additional paid-content access. Cancellation or inactivity while the account remains open neither cancels the support commitment nor selects a Creator. There is no voluntary right to cash-out, redeem or request return of carry merely because it is unused, paid service has ended or payment is preferred to allocation. Account closure starts the one-year preservation and contest-fallback process, not an automatic monetary refund. Disclose this nature, reopening deadline and fallback with the subscription’s Fund and closure information. Mandatory remedies and previously accrued rights under an earlier accepted version remain.

4.3 On account closure, including subscriber-initiated closure, stop scoring at the identified lawful cutoff, resolve valid pre-cutoff activity under Section 4.6, and preserve the verified unallocated creator-support amount separately in the Fund records for one calendar year from that cutoff. The period ends at the same UTC time on its first anniversary; February 29 uses February 28 in the following year. No new activity, fee or discretionary reduction consumes it. Give closure confirmation stating the amount, deadline, supported reopening route and contest fallback, with a reminder at least 30 days before expiry where a lawful usable contact route remains. Reopening remains subject to actual eligibility and reasonable review; no new platform subscription purchase is required solely to restore carry. A timely supported request pauses contest allocation during necessary verification or good-faith review; Shobble’s delay cannot defeat restoration. Approved reopening restores the remaining amount for subsequent qualifying allocation, not cash payment. Existing Creator commitments are excluded. Mandatory law and Section 4.8 take priority.

4.4 No ordinary carry refund is created by closure, permanent loss of program eligibility or Fund termination. For a permanent end of eligibility or the Fund without account closure, apply the same one-year preservation and contest-fallback rules to the unallocated support amount, measured from the identified permanent-participation cutoff; this does not itself close the general account. Where law or an existing separately accepted obligation requires a refund, redemption or other return, provide it regardless of the preservation period or proposed contest use. Initiate any undisputed return owed under this Schedule within 30 calendar days of its applicable trigger, sooner if required. A legally required remedy requested by an identifiable participant runs from receipt under its actual deadline; verification does not restart it. Delay only genuinely affected amounts under a necessary restriction or unresolved ownership/calculation issue, with explanation and review. Failed returns remain owed; no duplicate recovery or unauthorized offset applies.

4.5 Track carry by original funding and service portions; apply older unallocated portions first unless a correction targets another transaction. Valid original-payment refunds/reversals adjust only the corresponding unallocated support amount without duplicate recovery. No amount may be counted simultaneously as open carry, a closed-account preserved amount, a contest contribution, compensation committed to a Creator or a legally payable return. Carry cannot be sold, transferred between users, spent as a general wallet or voluntarily withdrawn. Preserve actual legal obligations and prior accrued rights; required cash returns and Creator settlements use Section 6’s residual rule, while contest-pool aggregation follows Section 4.7.

4.6 The ordinary allocation cutoff is the end of the calendar month in UTC, immediately before the next begins. Before it, scores and recipient proportions are provisional; the first event does not fix a Creator’s dollar award. At cutoff, positive Total Score establishes Creator allocation entitlement against the eligible Available Budget under Section 2.4; zero Total Score leaves the support unallocated as carry. Monthly Finalization validates inputs and amount subject to Section 8, not a new discretionary decision whether compensation is honored. An earlier lawful closure, permanent-ineligibility or program-termination cutoff ends that window and uses valid activity and funded service through it. Identify the cutoff without undue delay; ordinary processing, retained legal records or verification cannot admit later activity consuming an amount due for preservation or a legally required return. Account separately for remaining paid service and actual remedies. No scores accrue during closure; eligible post-reopening activity can allocate restored carry. Bank transfer/internal approval is not the entitlement event.

4.7 If the account has not reopened at the one-year deadline, and no timely reopening review or superior legal obligation prevents it, apply the legally eligible remaining amount to the prize pool of the next Shobble-sponsored creator contest offered afterward. This selects the disclosed alternative creator-support route for that amount but does not itself pay prizes or discharge remaining prize obligations; it is not forfeiture to Shobble’s operating revenue, an administration charge or a declaration that money owned by a subscriber has been abandoned. Preserve exact fractions when aggregating; the contribution is not an individual cash settlement requiring upward rounding. Keep it earmarked while awaiting the next contest. Published official rules govern lawful entry, selection and prizes; contribution creates no automatic entry, chance, advantage or prize for the former subscriber. Account for it once as funded prizes without administration/marketing deductions or relabeling ordinary expenditure. If the contest is cancelled, preserve the contribution for the next eligible sponsored contest. Reopening after a valid completed allocation does not automatically restore it. If no further contest will be provided, preserve and lawfully discharge the support commitment rather than appropriate it for operations.

4.8 The contractual framing describes the intended bargain; it does not by itself determine statutory property status, beneficial ownership, tax treatment or payment regulation. Contest use is permitted only where applicable law and actual accepted payment arrangements allow it. If an amount is legally property owed to another person or subject to a required refund, redemption, safeguarding, binding claim or unclaimed-property duty, apply that obligation first. The anniversary is a restoration deadline and allocation condition, not a substitute statutory abandonment period. Isolate supported unresolved legal/ownership disputes for proper review. If contest use is not lawful, preserve and discharge the amount by its required lawful route. Apply changes only to scope validly accepted; no prior accrued return entitlement is retrospectively removed. Actual successor/representative authority applies to death or incapacity.

5. Measurement and activity limits

5.1 Appendix A is part of this version. No other activity, undisclosed dwell threshold or additional global Creator/day cap applies. Identify the primary content type before scoring; the same event cannot earn duplicate media awards. Distinct genuine activities may add unless the book/comic higher-of rule applies. Replicated works, mirrors, cosmetic editions and technical retries do not reset caps. A materially new edition is a separate object only if consistently identified before scoring.

5.2 A detail-page view requires intentional user navigation, successful delivery and active display/accessibility presentation of the work’s supported representation. Feeds, previews, preloads, hidden tabs and automatic requests do not qualify. A separate expanded artwork viewer qualifies as full-screen where it presents the supported full-size work, including mobile/accessibility equivalents; operating-system full-screen mode is unnecessary. One event of each stated type can count per work/user/UTC day; refreshes and toggles add nothing.

5.3 Downloads require completed authorized delivery of the selected product/edition or complete supported variant, confirmed by reliable delivery/receipt evidence. A link, start, redirect or referral alone is insufficient; a third-party handoff needs completion confirmation. Interrupted transfers qualify only on completion. One logical package earns one award regardless of chunks/files/resolutions; optional alternatives need not all be downloaded. A page qualifies when its canonical content is presented in the active reader/accessibility equivalent and a user-originated action—navigation, scrolling into it, an accessibility reading command, annotation or selection—establishes presentation. Prefetch and automatic loops alone do not qualify. No comprehension, eye-tracking or undisclosed reading-time test applies; speed alone does not establish abuse.

5.4 Register and disclose stable canonical pages before enabling their score. Reflow, font changes, panels, zoom and device screenfuls do not create pages; duplicated/blank insertion cannot inflate them. Continuous layouts require a supported stable map. Unsupported page routes must be identified; an eligible download can still score. Audio/video downloads alone earn no points under this version; offline playback requires reliable supported evidence.

5.5 Credit actual eligible playback seconds ÷ 20, including fractions and the event-time multiplier. Pauses, buffering, seeks, artificial time inflation and muted/zero-output audio do not count. Intentional background audio may count; background-only video does not. No microphone monitoring is required. Accessible captions, transcripts or visual reading use their actual enabled route, not an automatic abuse exclusion. The first qualifying traversal of a content passage counts actual real time: a 20-second passage at 2× speed earns 0.5 base points; at 0.5× speed it earns 2 if genuinely played. Same-day replay of the same passage adds nothing; genuine later-day replay may qualify. An earlier ineligible traversal has not consumed a credited traversal.

5.6 Concurrent timed players cannot each credit the full same clock interval. Use the subscriber’s selected primary player, otherwise the most recent user-originated play/resume/control action. Exact unresolved ties divide available elapsed seconds equally among qualifying tied players before multipliers. A video podcast uses one route for the same playback.

5.7 A first genuine accepted bid on a real commission offering scores once without requiring a win. Increases, extensions and technical restarts do not multiply awards. A valid losing bid, ordinary seller withdrawal or seller cancellation preserves the event. A bidder’s withdrawal preserves it only where the withdrawal complied with the auction terms and the original participation was genuine; an unauthorized retraction or bid placed solely to obtain points and evade the auction obligation does not qualify. Rejected, test, fabricated, unauthorized and own/controlled-account bids do not qualify; related persons may genuinely participate without collusion. Continued/copied nominal offerings are one auction; substantively separate real commissions may qualify separately. Offerings fabricated to farm points do not. A bidder needs the authority/capacity required by auction terms. Established seller fraud/ineligibility requires an attributable correction, not payment to an ineligible Creator or automatic blame on an innocent bidder; reconcile affected remaining scores or carry under Section 8.

6. Earning, precision and finalization

6.1 Compensation accrues as its applicable conditions are met, subject to valid adjustments. Before the allocation cutoff, Creator amounts are estimates. Section 4.6 establishes Creator entitlement and separates committed compensation from the remaining creator-support allocation; Monthly Finalization validates calculation and proper adjustments. Unpaid committed compensation is not also carry available for closure disposition. Administrative approval, funding/provider delay or batching does not make accrued compensation unearned. Maintain distinct open carry, closed-account preserved support, contest contributions, Creator commitments/finalized compensation and any actual return payables, without duplicate counting or prematurely ending carry-holder scoring eligibility.

6.2 Calculate at sufficient precision to preserve attributable fractions, using auditable residual controls. Do not round each event/day/subscriber share to cents, discard small shares, redirect them for payment-unit convenience or round every positive award upward. Aggregate Creator shares before whole-cent payment conversion and carry residuals for the same Creator without expiry. A $10 budget × 0.04% earns $0.004; 1,000 such shares earn $4. No one-cent minimum applies to earnings credit. Statements may round presentation only with accessible fractional detail; display does not extinguish entitlement.

6.3 Close the month on the first and complete ordinary validation/recording by the end of the third Business Day in UTC (Monthly Finalization). For Section 8’s loss boundary this deadline applies even if administration is late. Isolate targeted unresolved amounts rather than leaving the entire month provisional. A zero-score close preserves subscriber carry, not finalized Creator compensation. Necessary later settlement/evidence corrections are linked supplemental entries, not an extension of ordinary refund exposure. Business Day excludes weekends and U.S. federal banking holidays.

6.4 Apply legally required withholding/rounding separately and pay in supported minor currency units, including USD cents; remaining fractions stay owed without delaying tax legally due. On final lawful settlement, aggregate the role’s remaining same-currency balance and round a positive sub-cent residual upward once at Shobble’s separate cost. Keep subscriber and Creator obligations distinct; do not fund the addition from others’ shares. Necessary holds, verification and unclaimed processes do not cancel the underlying obligation.

7. Creator payouts and statements

7.1 Provide the finalized monthly statement and initiate payment of available eligible whole cents by the fifth Business Day, subject only to the disclosed batching rule or justified legal/accepted restriction. The ordinary threshold is $10 USD; smaller balances remain owed. At least every 90 days initiate all available whole cents even below $10, measured from the first unpaid available whole-cent amount since the last payment. Fraction-only balances remain until payable or finally settled. Closure does not forfeit them.

7.2 Before activation disclose the supported destination/method and expected provider delivery period. Shobble pays ordinary ACH costs. Optional faster, FX or alternative routes with a participant charge require separately disclosed accepted terms; no unspecified reserve, fee, minimum balance or interest applies. Allocation, initiation and bank delivery are distinct; Shobble’s own funding failure does not cancel its duties.

7.3 A failed/returned payout restores only the unpaid or actually returned net amount. Do not pay again remitted tax, amounts already received or otherwise discharged obligations. Link any withholding reversal/recovery separately. Ordinary failed-payment charges are Shobble’s unless a distinct attributable charge has an actual accepted/legal basis. Explain the failure and provide a correction route.

7.4 Creator statements distinguish period/version, earnings and accessible precision, opening/closing fractions, adjustments, withholding, available/held balances and initiated/completed/returned payments. Subscribers can see their own recipient Creators, amounts, periods, percentages, applied multipliers, budget/carry and adjustments. Distinguish estimates and allocation from bank receipt. Zero engagement shows support still unallocated, not an additional amount released to Shobble.

8. Adjustments, missing data and review

8.1 A valid refund/final reversal established by Monthly Finalization reduces the attributable current service price and shares, preserving earlier finalized compensation. After finalization Shobble bears ordinary subscription refunds, card fraud, chargebacks and provider charges without clawback from innocent Creators. This protects allocated compensation, not already refunded or source-adjusted unallocated carry. Material errors, duplicate payment or established attributable manipulation/breach may be corrected under actual authority. Cap recovery at the proper amount; no double deduction, unrelated bank debit or cross-person recovery.

8.2 Itemize affected transactions, periods, rules and reasons; preserve reasonable response and review. A provisional hold is not final liability. Restore amounts after successful challenge and correct underpayments without new sales, participation or unrelated acceptance. Customer remedies do not await the internal dispute. Refund accounting alone does not rescind protected licenses, permanent grants or surviving outputs.

8.3 Missing data is not zero engagement. First reconstruct reliably; then use an objectively reasonable, consistent supported estimate within the evidenced eligibility/relationship window. Representative same-period subscriber/Creator/work observations take priority; up to three preceding complete months may support it only after adjusting for changed relationships, releases, access and patterns. A follow, payment or download request alone is insufficient; do not mechanically apply history to new/different work. Explain material evidence/assumptions. If Shobble’s failure leaves no supportable estimate, supply a reasonable attributable Shobble-funded remedy, not invented scores or reduction of unrelated valid earnings. Ordinary allocations cannot exceed affected budgets; separate remedies avoid duplicate recovery.

8.4 Hold only reasonably affected amounts, give the lawful basis, release conditions and next review date where permitted, and review unresolved holds at least every 30 days. Pay undisputed available sums where reasonably possible. At support@shobble.com, identify the statement/period where possible. Acknowledge within 10 Business Days and ordinarily respond with reasons within 30 calendar days; explain justified extensions and give a further date. Permit human-capable review of material exclusions/calculations, not solely automated rejection. Good-faith disputes are not abuse; no forfeiture deadline for an unreported error is created. Legal notices: legal@shobble.com.

9. Privacy, tax and payment protections

9.1 Creators receive meaningful aggregate engagement/financial reporting, not contributors’ names, handles, IDs, lists, individual weights/amounts or identifying consumption histories. Subscriber self-access does not expose others’ activity. Combine, suppress or delay narrow reporting where reasonably needed to reduce identification through small groups/timestamps while preserving review. Do not promise mathematical anonymity; separately visible followers/members do not confer contribution access. Necessary lawful disclosures retain their scope; a Creator dispute alone does not authorize identifying contributors.

9.2 Necessary processing, retention, provider access and data rights follow the applicable Privacy Notice and lawful arrangements. This Schedule is not blanket consent to device tracking, unrelated profiling, sensitive inference, advertising reuse, AI training or identifiable video disclosures. Confidential detection details may be protected without defeating meaningful explanation or lawful review.

9.3 Participants must provide accurate required tax/payment documentation and updates. Shobble/providers retain statutory duties for their actual roles, classify actual compensation/source/recipient status and separately identify required withholding. No tax exemption, treaty result, net payment or universal form is promised. Credit, availability, accrual, withholding and delivery are separate; a threshold does not postpone statutory duties. Avoid erroneous duplicate deductions/reporting without preventing legally required reporting. Shobble’s income tax is not Creator withholding.

9.4 Actual custody/safeguarding and accepted provider arrangements govern their subjects; a Fund record or internal reserve alone promises no deposit, trust, escrow, insurance or interest. Shobble must perform and fund its designated creator-support commitment and keep accurate records of preserved and allocated amounts; unrelated expenditure or an accounting reclassification cannot discharge it. This Schedule does not by itself require a particular ownership or custody classification. Apply the legal, tax and accounting treatment supported by the actual arrangement: neither calling the subscription payment revenue nor earmarking it establishes an exemption, tax deferral or beneficial ownership. Cash movement, purpose changes and display rounding do not alone recognize an expense, discharge a payable or erase fractions. Mandatory duties apply where their actual conditions are met.

10. Changes, exit and survival

10.1 One accepted version, including Appendix A, governs each common subscriber calculation. Material economic/eligibility/payout changes follow actual notice and assent requirements, generally at least 30 days for materially adverse changes and longer where required. Changes ordinarily begin at a month boundary. A Creator declining a required successor may end future participation at the valid cutoff while retaining historical scores and earnings; incompatible individual versions cannot compete for the same budget.

10.2 A necessary urgent lawful legal/security change must identify prospective nonoverlapping subperiods, with one common version within each, preserving history and required notice/assent. Apportion new service budget without overlap. Apply opening carry once, in chronological order to the first positive-score subperiod, carrying it onward if none qualifies; never allocate the same carry twice. Urgency permits no unrelated retrospective economic change.

10.3 Participants may end future participation through supported settings/support. Identify the cutoff, honor paid customer commitments/remedies and account for eligible pre-cutoff activity, Creator compensation, preserved support, contest contributions, legally owed returns, justified holds and final settlement. Closure preservation and contest fallback concern only support not already committed to Creators, never their earned compensation. Refusal of unrelated/new terms does not cancel accepted obligations. Closure, death/incapacity, failed details or inability to contact a participant does not itself release Shobble from the agreed creator-support purposes; use authorized successors and applicable legal processes where needed. Protected acquisitions, surviving fulfillment, necessary evidence, review and payment duties continue for their proper purposes.
