2. INFORMATION AND SOURCES

We collect information you provide, information generated through your use, and necessary information from authorized representatives, institutions, transaction counterparties and service providers. Collection depends on the actual feature and your role.

2.1 Account and profile information: account identifiers, contact details, authentication records, disclosed age/eligibility attributes, profile text/images, settings and optional links. Public profile fields and private account information are different. Do not put identity documents, private contact information or payment credentials in public fields.

2.2 Content and communications: uploaded works and metadata, listings, rights and provenance evidence, messages, comments, reviews, support requests, reports and appeals. Entrusted private files may include notes, saves, annotations, project files, assessment submissions and associated metadata where those features are supplied. A public submission and a private workspace do not have the same audience or permitted uses.

2.3 Transactions and creator records: orders, purchases, license and Acquisition Record references, accepted document versions and assent evidence, memberships, bids and commissions, amounts, taxes, refunds, disputes, payout records and required tax or business details. Records may identify the Holder, payer, representative, seller, Creator and authorized recipient separately.

2.4 Payment and payout information: payment-provider references and tokens, necessary limited payment-method details, authorization/settlement status, payout-destination information and verification or fraud results. Sensitive card, bank, identity and tax inputs use the designated provider or secure collection route. We disclose the recipient, necessary information and role at that collection step. Do not send full credentials or identity documents to ordinary support.

2.5 Verification information: necessary age, identity, representative-authority or creator/payment eligibility results and lawfully permitted compliance attributes. Government documents, selfies and liveness inputs go directly to the designated authorized verification provider rather than an ordinary support inbox or Acquisition Record. The provider's collection notice must explain actual inputs, any biometric processing, purposes, recipients, retention and rights. Shobble receives only necessary disclosed results and attributes that law permits it to receive and retain.

2.6 Activity and technical information: content and page identifiers, views, full-screen events, reading and listening/watch duration, downloads, searches, follows, membership status, bids, session timestamps, IP addresses, browser/device information, authentication events, error records and security signals. We use only the subset necessary for the actual purpose. A statutory or contractual collection limit still applies to a technical identifier or inference.

2.7 Managed-access information: institution or organization identity, actual representative authority, eligible participant references, role and roster assignments, applicable Capacity, reassignment and recovery history, and necessary grant evidence. Management does not itself authorize collection of unrelated personal activity or private accounts.

2.8 Shobble does not collect a sexual-content profile, sexual-content blacklist, or restricted sexual-content activity record.

2.9 External sources: payment and verification providers supply necessary results; authorized managers provide roster or authority records; counterparties provide fulfillment or dispute information; security services supply necessary threat signals. Connected services provide only information within the connection you authorize. Public availability does not justify unrestricted harvesting or use. Where law requires notice about indirectly obtained data, we identify its source, categories and purpose within the required period.

3. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

3.1 We use necessary account and authentication information to establish and secure accounts, administer access and provide the requested service. Where applicable, our basis is performance of the contract with the individual, a legal obligation, or a documented legitimate interest in secure service delivery. A contract with an institution does not automatically make contract performance the basis for processing every learner's data.

3.2 We use content, rights records and technical delivery data to host and supply authorized material, maintain acquired libraries, administer actual license scope, support recovery and preserve valid recipient grants. The basis is the applicable contractual necessity, legal duty or documented legitimate interest in fulfilling and evidencing authorized rights, as appropriate to the person and activity.

3.3 We use transaction, verification, tax and payout records to process actual purchases and payments, provide statements, administer renewals/cancellation and resolve errors, refunds and disputes. The basis is applicable contractual necessity, actual financial/tax duties or a documented legitimate interest in accurate accounting and fraud prevention. We do not claim that every participant or transaction is subject to identical KYC or AML obligations.

3.4 We use limited activity, eligibility and ledger records to administer the Creator Fund under Section 5. Where applicable, the basis is necessity to provide the subscribed service or creator program, or a documented legitimate interest in reliable allocation, fraud prevention and accounting. Optional tracking and processing of sensitive information need their own applicable basis and additional conditions; a program label supplies neither.

3.5 We use relevant reports, content, evidence and security signals to moderate, protect users, investigate abuse, enforce valid restrictions and provide appeals. The basis is an actual legal obligation or documented legitimate interest in safety, rights protection and service integrity. Processing special-category or criminal-offense information requires an applicable additional legal condition and safeguards; an accusation does not establish guilt.

3.6 We use communications to answer requests, provide necessary service notices and deliver optional updates you request. Marketing relies on consent where required or another specifically applicable lawful basis. You may unsubscribe or object to direct marketing without losing an acquisition. Necessary receipts, security and legal notices are separate from optional marketing.

3.7 Discovery, recommendations, analytics and reliability work use only information authorized for the actual operation and audience. Necessary service functions and optional analytics or personalization are distinguished at the feature or collection point. Nonessential device access/tracking uses consent where required; sensitive-data profiling and AI remain subject to Sections 6 and 8. This Policy is not authority for an undisclosed behavioral-advertising system.

3.8 Where we rely on legitimate interests, we assess necessity and balance those interests against the person's rights and reasonable expectations. Where we rely on consent, the request identifies the purpose and withdrawal route; you may withdraw as easily as giving it. Withdrawal does not retrospectively invalidate prior lawful processing. We do not substitute another basis merely to defeat a valid withdrawal or objection.

3.9 At collection, we identify whether information is required by law or necessary for the selected contract/feature, and the consequence of not providing it. Optional fields remain optional. If an essential lawful eligibility check cannot be completed, the affected feature may be unavailable; this does not itself cancel unrelated rights. A materially different new purpose requires notice and whatever additional authority, assessment or consent law requires before that use.

No unlimited storage or unoffered export capability is implied. A privacy request and the legal consequences of surrendering or rescinding a content grant are separate.

5. CREATOR FUND DATA AND VISIBILITY

5.1 For participating subscriptions, we process the applicable subscription period and revenue basis, follow or creator-membership relationship, Creator monetization eligibility, qualifying events, event-time membership weighting, validation and allocation records. Relevant events include covered artwork/asset views and downloads, video/music/podcast duration, book/comic reading/downloads and qualifying commission-auction bids, as specified in the accepted Fund Schedule. 5.2 Subscribers can see their own allocation recipients and amounts. 5.3 Access to individual Fund activity is restricted to necessary authorized service administration, validation, fraud review, payment, lawful requests and dispute correction. Any permitted disclosure outside ordinary reports must have its actual lawful and Schedule basis and remain limited. We do not publish allocation histories or provide contributor lists for Creator marketing.

5.4 The Fund Schedule's one-year closed-account preservation rule applies to the unallocated creator-support commitment, not a universal retention period for all viewing history. We retain only the identity/reference, amount, cutoff, restoration and other evidence necessary to operate that rule and meet actual legal duties. Later conditional contest application does not publish the former subscriber, enter them in the contest or transfer their engagement history to entrants. Earned Creator compensation is recorded separately.

10. RETENTION, DELETION AND CONTINUING RIGHTS

10.1 Retain personal information only for the identified purpose and necessary lawful period. Our documented schedule must distinguish the following categories; it cannot assign every category indefinite retention:

    Account/authentication records: while needed to provide the account and recover or secure it, followed by only the restricted period necessary for applicable obligations, actual disputes or justified abuse prevention.

    Acquisitions and grant evidence: while necessary to fulfill and substantiate permanent or unexpired rights, recipient grants and associated remedies, with unnecessary profile or activity details removed where possible. Permanent delivery does not justify permanent retention of every usage event.

    Financial/tax/payout records: for the specific applicable tax, accounting, payment, reporting, limitation or legal-hold period, measured from the relevant transaction or event. Unpaid compensation and unresolved claims remain distinct from ordinary analytics.

    Verification records: only the minimum result or evidence legally permitted and needed for the actual eligibility, provider or compliance purpose. Raw inputs remain subject to the provider's disclosed period and any stricter local deletion rule. No universal five-year or indefinite identity-document retention applies.

    Private files and submissions: for the supplied feature and agreed retention/exit process, plus only necessary restricted fulfillment, evidence or legal retention. Assessment, publication, source delivery and account recovery have different purposes.

    Activity/Fund validation records: for the relevant calculation, fraud review and correction/appeal window, then delete or appropriately aggregate details no longer necessary. Preserve required allocation, compensation and carry records separately.

    Security, moderation, support and appeal records: for the active issue and proportionate documented prevention, review, legal or limitation period, with reassessment of continuing need.

    Optional marketing/analytics records: until the authorized purpose ends, consent is withdrawn or a valid objection applies, subject to any separate necessary lawful retention. Keep only minimal suppression information where needed to honor an opt-out.

10.2 Where a definite period is required or can be given for a collection, its specific notice must state that period; otherwise it must explain meaningful criteria and the event from which retention is measured. Provider-specific deletion and backup cycles must be disclosed where material. A legal hold must identify an actual basis, restrict access/use and end when its justification expires.

10.3 We delete or irreversibly deidentify data when its lawful retention ends and instruct restricted providers accordingly. Backups may follow a documented deletion cycle, but retained copies remain access-restricted and unavailable for unrelated processing; restoration must reapply effective deletion/opt-out instructions. A local rule requiring faster deletion controls. We do not promise immediate complete overwriting of every medium.

10.4 Account closure does not require cancellation of surviving acquisitions or erase payment obligations. We explain necessary remaining records, applicable exit retrieval and consequences, and retain only what is required to fulfill or evidence the actual surviving scope. We do not condition every privacy request on surrendering a license. Where a requested deletion necessarily prevents a selected service, explain that specific effect and lawful alternatives rather than assert a universal forfeiture.
