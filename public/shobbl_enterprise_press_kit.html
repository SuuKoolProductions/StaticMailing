executive summary

Infrastructure for creator-owned businesses.

Shobbl replaces the duct-taped creator stack with one owned home: shop, memberships, audience, archive, and payout history in the same place. The press story is not another feature tour. It is a market shift toward creator identity, ownership, portability, and consent.
