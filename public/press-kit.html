About Shobble

Shobble is a social platform for human creativity. It brings creator discovery, digital storefronts, memberships, and community together, so artists, musicians, writers, game developers, and filmmakers can share their work, sell digital releases, and build relationships with the people who support them. Creators retain ownership of their work, and generative AI outputs are not allowed. Shobble is operated by Shobbl Incorporated.

what makes shobble different

One home for the work, the shop, and the people.

Creators usually split their work across a feed, a storefront, a membership service, and a chat server. Shobble puts those in one place, with rules written down.

All in one

Share, sell, and run memberships from one account

Posts, art, music, video, games, writing, commissions, and memberships live together. Fans can follow, buy, and join without leaving the platform.

Ownership

Creators retain ownership of their work

Creators keep all right, title, and interest in their content. Shobble receives only the limited licenses described in the Creator Content License.

Human-made

No generative AI outputs

Creator verification and a written AI policy keep the platform focused on work made by people.

Protection

Anti-scraping measures

Client-side encryption, rate limiting, and bot management make it harder to harvest creator work in bulk for training sets or mirrored archives.

Policy

Clear policies

Clear policies explain what's allowed, how enforcement works, and how to appeal. All of them are public at shobble.com/policies.

Memberships

Creators set the tiers

Creators choose their own membership levels, perks, and prices, from free followers to paid supporters.
