# Shobble

Landing page for Shobble, a creator-owned social platform for artists, musicians, game developers, writers, and independent makers.

The page introduces storefronts, memberships, communities, and payouts, and includes a press kit at `/press-kit` (served from `public/press-kit.html`; logo downloads and self-hosted fonts live in `public/press/`). Privacy, Terms of Use, and the incorporated policy addendums from the live platform are published at `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/policies` and linked from the footer. Bug reports are collected at `/submit-a-bug` and delivered with `SLACK_BUG_REPORT_WEBHOOK_URL`.
