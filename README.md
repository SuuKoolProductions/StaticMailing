# Shobble

Landing page for Shobble, a creator-owned marketplace for artists, musicians, game developers, writers, and independent makers.

The page introduces storefronts, memberships, communities, and payouts, and includes an enterprise press kit at `/shobble_enterprise_press_kit.html`. Privacy, Terms of Use, and the incorporated policy addendums from the live platform are published at `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/policies` and linked from the footer.
